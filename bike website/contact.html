Frequently Asked Questions

How long does delivery take?

Delivery typically takes 5-7 business days across India. We provide real-time tracking so you can monitor your bike's delivery status.

Can I return a bike after purchase?

Yes, we have a 7-day return policy if the bike is unused and in original condition. Contact our support team for details.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept EMI, Credit/Debit Cards, Net Banking, UPI, and Cash on Delivery. Choose the option that works best for you.

Do you provide warranty?

Yes, all bikes come with 2-3 years manufacturer warranty. We also offer extended warranty options for additional protection.

Can I modify my bike after purchase?

Yes, we provide guidance on modifications. Our experts can help with safe modifications that don't void warranty.
